SENTRY-Africa

Support

SENTRY-Africa (Sepsis Evaluation and Risk Tracking) is a bedside reference aid for clinicians triaging hospitalized adults with suspected sepsis in settings where sepsis burden is high.

Questions, feedback, or a problem with the app?
Open an issue at github.com/jordan-1u/sepsis-risk-calculator/issues.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the UVA score come from?
The Universal Vital Assessment score was derived and validated in hospitalized adults in sub-Saharan Africa: Moore CC, et al. BMJ Global Health. 2017;2(2):e000344.

How reliable is the 14-day mortality estimate?
It comes from a model fitted in a derivation cohort and has not yet been externally validated or peer reviewed. Treat it as exploratory. The app's About screen describes the model and its limitations.

Does the app store patient information?
No. Everything is calculated on your device and nothing is saved or sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Disclaimer

SENTRY-Africa is intended for trained healthcare professionals as a reference aid. It is not a diagnostic device and does not replace clinical judgment.
