SENTRY-Africa

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

SENTRY-Africa does not collect, store, or share any data.

What the app does with what you enter

Values you enter, such as vital signs, UVA scores, lactate values and IV fluid volumes, are used only to perform calculations on your device. They are not saved after the app is closed and are never transmitted off the device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data
  • No network requests; the app works fully offline

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/jordan-1u/sepsis-risk-calculator/issues.
